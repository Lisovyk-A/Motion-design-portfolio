SubLook · Personal Subscription Tracker

Privacy Policy

SubLook is designed to help you track your personal subscriptions while keeping data handling minimal. Information you enter is processed primarily on your device and is not used for advertising, profiling, or data resale.

Contact

02

Information We Process

When you use SubLook, the app may process information that you choose to enter, including:

  • subscription names;
  • plan names;
  • categories;
  • prices and currencies;
  • billing cycle details;
  • upcoming billing dates;
  • trial end dates;
  • subscription end dates;
  • notes;
  • visual customization choices, such as colors, emojis, and optional custom images.

The app may also store certain local preferences, such as your selected currency and notification settings.

03

How We Use Information

SubLook uses this information only to provide the app's functionality, including:

  • saving and displaying your subscriptions;
  • calculating upcoming charges;
  • showing payment schedules and calendar views;
  • creating local reminders for upcoming payments or trial endings;
  • displaying your chosen subscription artwork and customization;
  • supporting optional in-app tip purchases through Apple.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling data to third parties.

04

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your subscription data is stored locally on your device.

If iCloud is available for your Apple account and device, SubLook may also sync your data through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. In that case, Apple processes that synced data under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

We do not have direct access to your personal iCloud account or the contents of your iCloud storage.

05

Photos and Images

If you choose to add a custom image to a subscription, SubLook accesses only the image you select through Apple's system photo picker.

Selected images are stored locally on your device and may also be included in iCloud sync through Apple's services if syncing is available. We do not upload your photos to our own servers.

06

Notifications

If you enable reminders, SubLook may request permission to send notifications.

When enabled, reminders are generated locally on your device. These notifications may include information such as the subscription name, amount, or a reminder that a payment or trial end date is approaching.

We do not send notification content to our own servers.

07

In-App Purchases

SubLook may offer optional tip purchases through Apple's In-App Purchase system using StoreKit.

Payment processing, billing information, and related transaction data are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details or banking information.

08

Data We Do Not Collect

SubLook does not use:

  • third-party analytics SDKs;
  • advertising SDKs;
  • cross-app or cross-site tracking;
  • advertising identifiers for targeted advertising;
  • data brokers or marketing platforms.
09

Legal Basis for Processing

If you are located in a jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing personal data, we process information on the following bases:

  • to provide the functionality of the app;
  • based on your consent, where you grant access to photos or notifications;
  • where necessary to respond to support requests you send to us.
10

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device, and where applicable in your iCloud account, until you delete it.

You can delete your data by:

  • deleting subscriptions inside the app;
  • removing the app from your device;
  • managing or deleting related iCloud data through your Apple account settings.

If you contact us by email, we may retain your contact information and message for as long as necessary to respond to your request and manage support communications.

11

Security

We aim to minimize the data we handle and rely on Apple's platform security, local device storage, and Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure where applicable.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12

Children's Privacy

SubLook is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data, or to object to certain processing.

Because SubLook stores most data locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account, many of these actions can be performed directly by you through the app and your Apple settings.
