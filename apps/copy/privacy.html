CopY · Clipboard Manager for Mac

Privacy Policy

CopY stores your clipboard history on your device so you can reuse copied text and images. Clipboard data is processed locally on your Mac and is not used for analytics, tracking, or advertising.

Effective date · April 3, 2026
01

Data Collection

CopY does not collect or transmit your clipboard history to our servers. The app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

02

Clipboard Data

CopY reads your clipboard to save a history of copied items, including text and images. This information is stored locally on your device at:

  • ~/Library/Application Support/CopY/

By default, this clipboard data remains on your device and is not shared with us or with third parties, except when you choose to enable iCloud sync as described below.

03

Accessibility Access

CopY requests Accessibility access solely to paste clips back into the previously active application. This permission is not used for any other purpose, and no data from other apps is collected through it.

04

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, CopY may sync your clipboard history across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to the developer. You can disable iCloud sync in System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

05

Third-Party Services

CopY does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising tools.

06

Data Retention & Deletion

You control your clipboard history entirely. You can delete individual clips or clear all history at any time from within the app. If you want to remove CopY's local data manually, delete the folder at ~/Library/Application Support/CopY/.

07

Children's Privacy

CopY does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

08

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
